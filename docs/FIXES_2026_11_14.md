# Cóndor Rojo — recovery patch

## Changes
- Accessibility setting in Options: no filter, high contrast, blue/yellow palette, grayscale; saved in `user://settings.cfg`.
- `Tab: INTI` hint uses 11 px text and a smaller backing label.
- Astronaut walking speed is 40 world px/s, sprint multiplier remains 1.28. Running suit oxygen consumption is slightly above running energy consumption.
- Time moves continuously across dawn, and the sky brightens gradually before 06:00. Sleeping opens the night journal; without sleep, dawn automatically advances to the next sol and applies a half-ration.
- A visible emergency oxygen reservoir is near the lander. Module oxygen tanks also work. Stand near a tank and press E/Interact to refill over 2 seconds. A depleted reservoir needs 120 in-game minutes to replenish; refill transfers only the oxygen needed and conserves the remainder.
- Hold E/Interact while walking towards the rover to push it, with a gentle wheel/body rocking motion.
- Touch controls: compact 2x2 action cluster and separated pause/+ above the minimap, joystick bottom-left; obsolete saved layouts are reset once.
- Resizing: viewport aspect expansion and dynamic world render size avoid black side bars and horizontally stretched world graphics.
- Custom web loader retained.

## Manual smoke checks
1. Godot 4.7: open project, let it import, press F6 or run MainMenu. Confirm no parse errors.
2. Options (main menu AND pause): pick accessibility mode, close and reopen it, restart and confirm it persists.
3. Start a new game: finish placing modules; on surface walk with WASD (40 px/s), sprint with Shift, jump with Space.
4. Press Tab: compact hint disappears; INTI dialogue should still open/close normally.
5. At the spare oxygen tank near the lander: deplete some O2, press E (or touch hand), see 2-second refill and reserve decrease; repeat to confirm only remaining stock is used.
6. Hold E and walk into rover to push it. Let go to stop it.
7. Allow the clock to reach 05:00: next sol should begin without waiting for a sleep/journal action; lighting brightens before dawn.
8. Export to Web from the included preset, resize browser from 16:9 to wide and narrow landscape; HUD, minimap, and on-screen controls should remain accessible and world pixels should not stretch horizontally.
9. Test real touch input in landscape and touch-control edit/save mode. New default positions apply once when upgrading from a legacy layout.

Note: A source-project patch requires a fresh Godot export; its HTML files are not precompiled in this ZIP.
