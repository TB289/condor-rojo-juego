# Cóndor Rojo — revised 10-sol progression (v2.5.38)

This is a **game scenario**. Real NASA landing sites and mission dates are described in rover cards, but the player's map and weather sequence are fictional.

| Sol | Fixed event / unlocked story chapter |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Land at Isidis Planitia; plant Ecuadorian flag; place solar panel. Clock starts at 06:00 after placement. At **10:00** INTI warns of a dust storm; at **11:00** the interactive sandstorm begins (five in-game hours after setup). Night journal reviews storm damage. |
| 2 | Clean/repair equipment; water-ice search, first mineral study and supply sorting unlock. Night journal warns of tomorrow's solar-radiation storm. |
| 3 | Solar-radiation storm at roughly mid-afternoon: INTI countdown, vivid visual effects and dangerous surface exposure. Greenhouse potato mission can start after ice research. |
| 4 | Isidis rover recovery (Spirit and Opportunity at opposite ends) and the habitat log become available. |
| 5 | Jezero biosignature research unlocks after mineral study. Crops can be harvested as objectives allow. |
| 6 | Cave exploration unlocks, including Sojourner tracks and the cave archive. Meteor journal events may begin. |
| 7 | Jezero mineral samples and glacier core exploration unlock. **No major scripted storm** on Sol 7. |
| 8 | Five-rover archives and greenhouse maintenance become available. |
| 9 | Optional communications and spare-equipment maintenance become available. **No solar storm** on Sol 9. |
| 10 | Complete remaining objectives and reach the normal end-of-mission debrief, with outcomes depending on decisions and survival. |

`min_sol` gates **unlocking only**, not completion deadlines. The player may finish unlocked missions later. Any mission can also have existing objective prerequisites that must be satisfied. Timings are game-clock times, not real-life hours.
