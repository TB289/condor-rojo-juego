# Cóndor Rojo hotfix — cave, oxygen, sprint, INTI and resizing

Changes in this ZIP:

- Cave entrance: larger Area2D plus nearby Interact fallback and cyan landmark lamp. Use **E** next to the cave mouth; inside, use E at the exit; mission progression remains tied to entering the cave.
- Camera: confines the visible surface to a safe interior rectangle; world layout still expands without changing pixel aspect ratio. Main menu background texture now fills wider/taller windows instead of leaving a black strip.
- Walking: 40 px/second; sprint while holding Shift, touch sprint or configured controller action moves at 66 px/second outdoors. Sprint consumes energy.
- Oxygen: functional refill station only inside the HABITAT, beside the interior wall. Approach and press E. Transfers the needed amount over 2 seconds, retains unused stock, and recharges stock over 120 in-game minutes, continuing while exploring elsewhere. The outside emergency tank and automatic interior oxygen refill were removed. The Safe Kit oxygen module is installed as habitat equipment, not placed outside; it still generates oxygen per sol if powered.
- INTI: narrower dock and smaller portrait with larger text, tip and close button; the compact journal version stays separate.
- Mission progress strip: hidden outside timed work; actual work progress used during scans and repairs.
- The custom branded Web loader, touch controls, accessibility filters, and rover remain in the project.

3. Navigate north to the cave; observe cyan marker and press E. Confirm the cave interior loads and mission advances; leave and re-enter.
4. Test wide and tall native windows and mobile browser, including menu: no empty world strip or stretched character art.
5. Press Tab on a long INTI dialogue and confirm it is legible, narrower, and the close button works.
